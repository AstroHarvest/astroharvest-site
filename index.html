AstroHarvest

Mine the void. Never stop climbing.

A cosy pixel-art idle game about hauling ore out of deep space. Fly, mine, upgrade, and push through eleven sectors — and keep earning while your phone's in your pocket.

Coming soon to Android

What it is

Mine three resources

Ore, gas and crystal, each with its own nodes, its own rhythm and its own upgrade path. Sell it all for gold and put the gold straight back into the ship.

Progress while you're away

Set the autopilot, put the phone down, come back to a pile of resources. The game is built to be walked away from — not to punish you for leaving.

Eleven sectors to climb

Each one further out, richer and meaner than the last. Reaching the deep end is a journey measured in months, not an afternoon.

A meta game underneath

Prestige runs, drone fleets, catalyst crafting, node mastery and a crew who actually turn up when you call. There is always something worth opening.

How it's built

No pay-to-win

Nothing sold will ever let someone buy their way up the ladder past you. That is a design rule, not a launch promise.

Nothing is collected

No accounts, no analytics, no trackers, no ads. Your save lives on your phone and goes nowhere else. See the privacy policy.

Plays offline

Every image, every note of music and every sound is bundled in the app. No connection needed to play.
