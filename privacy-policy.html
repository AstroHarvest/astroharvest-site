AstroHarvest — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026

The short version: AstroHarvest does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. Your game progress is saved on your own device and never sent anywhere. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the AstroHarvest mobile game (package com.astroharvest.app), referred to below as "the game". The game is developed and published by an independent developer at astroharvestgame.com; contact details are at the end of this page.

Information we collect

None. The game does not collect personal information of any kind. Specifically, it does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any other contact details
  • Your location
  • Your device's advertising identifier
  • Contacts, photos, files, microphone, or camera data
  • Usage analytics, behavioural data, or crash reports

The game has no user accounts and no login. You are never asked to identify yourself.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress — resources, upgrades, unlocked sectors, settings, and similar gameplay state — in your device's local browser-style storage inside the app. This data:

  • never leaves your device
  • is not readable by us or by anyone else
  • contains no personal information, only gameplay values

You can erase it at any time using the wipe-save option in the game's settings, or by clearing the app's storage through your device settings, or by uninstalling the game. Uninstalling removes it permanently, and it cannot be recovered.

Network use

The game runs entirely offline. All artwork, music, and sound are bundled inside the app itself and loaded from local storage. The game does not contact any server we operate, and we operate no server that receives player data.

Third-party services

The game currently includes no third-party software development kits — no advertising networks, no analytics providers, no crash-reporting services, and no social media integrations.

The game is distributed through Google Play. Google collects certain information as part of operating that store, independently of us and under its own privacy policy. We do not receive that information in any individually identifiable form.

Children

AstroHarvest is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone — including children — because, as described above, the game does not collect personal information at all.

Your rights

Privacy laws in many regions give you the right to access, correct, export, or delete personal data a company holds about you. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to retrieve or erase. The only data connected to the game is the save file on your own device, which is entirely under your control.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects privacy — for example if in-app purchases, advertising, or crash reporting are added in a future version — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the "last updated" date above will change. Significant changes will also be noted in the release notes on the game's store listing.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about privacy in AstroHarvest can be sent to: beat@astroharvestgame.com
